--- id: export-docx
**File ▸ Export output… ▸ Word (.docx)**.

Builds an **editable** Word report from the Output pane: tables become real Word tables and plots are embedded as images. Formatting, renaming and reordering are then ordinary Word work.

Choose this when the output is going into a document you will keep writing — a thesis chapter, a manuscript, a report. For something that should look identical everywhere and need no application, [HTML](howto:export-html) is the simpler artefact.
