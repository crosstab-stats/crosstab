--- id: import-wikipedia
**File ▸ Import data… ▸ Wikipedia table…**, then paste an article URL or title and pick which table you want.

Handy for teaching and for quick reference data — country populations, league tables, historical lists — without leaving the app or hand-copying anything.

Treat what arrives as a draft. Wikipedia tables carry footnote markers, merged header rows, ranges and the occasional "n/a", so a column that should be numeric often is not until you have cleaned it. Check the [Variables tab](howto:variable-types) before analysing, and remember the source is editable by anyone and worth citing as of a date.
